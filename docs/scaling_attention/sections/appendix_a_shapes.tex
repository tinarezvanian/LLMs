\chapter{Tensor shape cheat sheet}
\label{app:shapes}

A single-page reference for the tensor shapes you'll see in any transformer
or transformer-adjacent code. Use it when reading other people's
implementations. \emph{Bytes} are for fp16 (2~bytes/element) with batch
\(B=1\); multiply by \(B\) for larger batches.

\section*{Dense transformer (Llama-3 8B--class numerics for illustration)}

Take \(n = 8{,}192\) tokens, \(\dmodel = d = 4{,}096\), \(\heads = 32\),
\(\dhead = 128\), \(d_{\text{ff}} = 14{,}336\), \(V = 128{,}256\). FlashAttention
never materialises the full \(n\times n\) tensor in HBM; the shape still
exists \emph{abstractly} for FLOP accounting.

\begin{table*}[htbp]
\centering
\scriptsize
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{3pt}
\begin{tabular}{@{}llllr@{}}
\toprule
Tensor & Symbol & Shape & Where & Bytes (fp16) \\
\midrule
Token ids & \(x\) & \([B, n]\) & dataloader & \(B\cdot n\cdot 4\) (int32) \\
Embedded tokens & \(X\) & \([B, n, d]\) & embed lookup & \(B\cdot n\cdot d\cdot 2\) \\
\(Q\) (one head) & \(Q_h\) & \([B, n, \dhead]\) & projection & \(\approx 2.0\,\mathrm{MiB}\) \\
\(K\) / \(V\) (one head) & \(K_h, V_h\) & \([B, n, \dhead]\) & projection & \(\approx 2.0\,\mathrm{MiB}\) each \\
Attention logits & \(S_h\) & \([B, n, n]\) & matmul (often \emph{not} stored) & \(\approx 128\,\mathrm{MiB}\) if materialised \\
Attention weights & \(A_h\) & \([B, n, n]\) & softmax output & same order \\
Head output & \(O_h\) & \([B, n, \dhead]\) & \(A_h V_h\) & \(\approx 2.0\,\mathrm{MiB}\) \\
MHA block output & --- & \([B, n, d]\) & concat\(+\)proj & \(B\cdot n\cdot d\cdot 2\) \\
FFN up-projection & --- & \([B, n, d_{\text{ff}}]\) & SwiGLU intermediate & \(\approx 224\,\mathrm{MiB}\) \\
Logits & \(Z\) & \([B, n, V]\) & lm\_head & \(\approx 2.0\,\mathrm{GiB}\) \\
\midrule
KV cache (1 layer, MHA) & --- & \(2 \times [n, d]\) & inference RAM & \(2\cdot n\cdot d\cdot 2\) \(\approx 128\,\mathrm{MiB}\) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Order-of-magnitude byte counts for one representative configuration.
The attention \(n^{2}\) cells dominate \emph{activation} memory when stored;
FlashAttention avoids storing \(S_h\) and \(A_h\) wholesale.}
\label{tab:shape-master}
\end{table*}

\noindent
The \(Q/K/V\) row lists \emph{per head}; multiply by \(\heads\) if you stack
before viewing.

\section*{KV-cache layout (Llama-3 70B snapshot)}

Llama-3 70B uses \(L = 80\) layers, \(\heads = 64\), GQA with \(n_{\text{kv}} = 8\)
repeated key/value heads, \(\dhead = 128\). Per-token KV footprint scales with
\(2\,L\,n_{\text{kv}}\,\dhead\) rather than \(2\,L\,\heads\,\dhead\).

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lccc@{}}
\toprule
Variant & Key dims / tok & Value dims / tok & fp16 B/tok \\
\midrule
MHA (hyp., 64 heads) & \(64 \cdot 128\) & \(64 \cdot 128\) & \(\approx 65{,}536\) \\
GQA-8 (actual 70B) & \(8 \cdot 128\) & \(8 \cdot 128\) & \(\approx 8{,}192\) \\
MQA (1 KV head) & \(128\) & \(128\) & \(\approx 1{,}024\) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Per-token KV footprint \emph{per layer} scales linearly with the
number of KV heads. GQA sits between MHA and MQA; Llama-3 70B uses GQA-8.}
\label{tab:kv-variants}
\end{table}

\noindent
Multiply Table~\ref{tab:kv-variants} by \(L\) and by sequence length \(n\) to
estimate total cache when serving a prompt.
