\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
This note collects the mathematics and systems reasoning behind long-context language models:
Kaplan/Chinchilla scaling laws, the $\bigO(n^2)$ cost of dense self-attention, the KV cache wall,
engineering mitigations (FlashAttention, sparse masks, hybrids), and the public framing of
Subquadratic's SSA mechanism.\footnote{Companion source material lives in
